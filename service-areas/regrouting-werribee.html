Young Houses, Tired Grout

Wyndham has been one of the fastest-growing parts of Victoria for twenty years. Tarneit, Point Cook, Truganina and Manor Lakes are mostly estate homes, and a lot of them are under fifteen years old.

That is plenty of time for builder-grade grout to fail. It cracks at the corners first. The silicone at the floor joint goes black. Then water gets in behind the tiles, and that is where the real damage starts.

Werribee itself is older, and its established streets have original bathrooms that have done their time. Different age, same fix. Strip it out, rebuild with Mapei, do it once.

FAQs - Wyndham

Common Questions

Do you actually come out to Wyndham?

Yes. Our installer is based in the Wyndham area, so Werribee, Tarneit and Point Cook are local work booked on normal timeframes. There is no call-out fee.

My house is under ten years old. Why is the shower already failing?

Because the grout and silicone were the cheapest that met spec. They pass handover, then crack at the corners and grow mould within a few years, even when the rest of the house is solid. We strip that material out and rebuild with Mapei, so it does not happen twice.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Werribee, Tarneit, Point Cook, Hoppers Crossing, Wyndham Vale, Truganina, Williams Landing, Manor Lakes and Little River are all standard service area.

Do my tiles need replacing?

Almost never. A regrout renews the grout lines and the silicone at the corners and floor joints. Your tiles stay where they are. We only replace a tile if it is cracked or loose.

How quickly can you start a Wyndham job?

Quotes within 24 hours. Standard booking lead time is one to two weeks. Being local, we can sometimes fit an urgent job in sooner.
